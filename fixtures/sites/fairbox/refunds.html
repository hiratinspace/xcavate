We want you to love everything you buy from FairBox. If something is not right, this page explains how to send it back. It is short on purpose.

1. About us

FairBox is a trading name of FairBox Home Goods Ltd., a company registered in Portland, Oregon. When these documents refer to "FairBox", "we", "us" or "our", they mean FairBox Home Goods Ltd. and, where the context requires, its employees and authorised agents.

We have been operating our online store since 2019 and we are proud of the community of customers who have trusted us along the way. Our team is small, and the people who answer your emails are the same people who choose what we stock and write these pages.

2. Our promise

If you are not happy with your purchase for any reason, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

You do not need to give us a reason, and items do not need to be in their original packaging, although we appreciate it when they are. Items you have used can be returned too, as long as they are clean.

3. Free returns

Returns are free: we email you a prepaid return label, so you never pay for return shipping.

We do not charge a restocking fee.

We never charge restocking fees, handling fees or any other deductions on returns.

You can drop your parcel at any post office or arrange a free collection from your home when you request the label.

4. When you will get your money back

Your refund goes back to your original payment method within 5 business days of your parcel reaching us.

We refund the full price you paid, including the original delivery charge if you paid one. We will email you as soon as the refund has been sent.

5. How to start a return

To start a return:

  1. open the email we sent when your order shipped, or sign in to your account;
  2. choose Return an item and tell us which items are coming back;
  3. print the prepaid label we send you, or show the QR code at the post office;
  4. pack the items and drop them off, or wait for the courier to collect them.

6. Faulty or damaged items

If anything arrives damaged or develops a fault, please tell us at any time. We will replace it or give you your money back, whichever you prefer, and we will cover all the postage. You will not need to send photos unless you want to.

7. Changing or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order online from your order page at any time before it ships. Once it has shipped, simply return it using the steps above when it arrives.

8. Gifts

If you received a FairBox item as a gift, you can return it in the same way. We will send the refund to the person who bought it, or give you a gift card for the same amount if you prefer, and we will not tell them that you returned it.

  1. "Website" means the FairBox website and any pages, tools and content made available on it.

  3. "Order" means a request by you to buy one or more products or services from us through the Website.
  4. "Business day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the state where we are registered.
  5. "Content" means text, images, video, software, logos and other material published on the Website.

10. Product information

We try hard to describe and photograph our products accurately. Colours can look slightly different on different screens, and handmade or natural materials vary a little from piece to piece. Measurements are given as a guide and may vary by a small amount.

If we discover an obvious error in a product description after you have ordered, we will contact you to explain and ask how you would like to proceed before we ship anything.
