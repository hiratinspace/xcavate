3. The StreamNest membership

A StreamNest membership gives you access to our full library of films, series and documentaries on up to four devices at the same time, in high definition where your connection allows. New titles are added every week and you can download episodes to watch offline on supported phones and tablets.

The Standard plan costs $19.99 per month, including applicable sales tax where we are required to collect it. We do not show advertising to members on any plan.

4. Free trial

New members can try StreamNest free for 7 days. You need to give us a valid payment card when you sign up so that your membership can continue without interruption after the trial. We place a temporary authorisation of $1.00 on the card to check that it is valid, and the authorisation is released by your bank within a few days.

At the end of your 7-day free trial, your membership will automatically convert to a paid monthly plan and you will be charged $19.99 per month.

Only one free trial is available per household, per payment card and per email address. We may decline a trial if we believe it has been used before.

5. Billing and renewal

Your subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel.

Your billing date is the day of the month on which your free trial ended. If that day does not exist in a given month, for example the 31st, we will bill you on the last day of that month instead. Charges appear on your statement as "STREAMNEST MEMBERSHIP".

If a payment fails we will try the card again over the following 7 days and email you so that you can update your details. Your access may be paused while a payment is outstanding.

6. Ending your membership

You can end your membership by following the cancellation procedure set out in our Terms of Service. If you end your membership, you keep access until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.

7. Gift cards and promotional codes

StreamNest gift cards can be redeemed in your account settings and are applied to your membership before your payment card is charged. Gift cards have no expiry date and cannot be exchanged for cash except where the law requires it.

Promotional codes are subject to the conditions printed on the offer and cannot be combined with a free trial unless the offer says so.

8. Devices and streaming quality

StreamNest works on most smart TVs, games consoles, phones, tablets and web browsers. The quality of what you watch depends on your internet connection and your device. We recommend at least 5 megabits per second for high definition. Not all titles are available in every quality.

The titles in our library change from time to time as licensing agreements begin and end. We list titles that are leaving the library at least two weeks in advance on the "Last chance" row.
