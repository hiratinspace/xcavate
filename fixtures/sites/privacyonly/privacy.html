We collect as little information as we can and we never sell it. This policy explains the details.

1. About us

PrivacyOnly is a trading name of PrivacyOnly Home Goods Ltd., a company registered in Portland, Oregon. When these documents refer to "PrivacyOnly", "we", "us" or "our", they mean PrivacyOnly Home Goods Ltd. and, where the context requires, its employees and authorised agents.

We have been operating our online store since 2019 and we are proud of the community of customers who have trusted us along the way. Our team is small, and the people who answer your emails are the same people who choose what we stock and write these pages.

2. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information PrivacyOnly collects when you visit the Website or buy from us, why we collect it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. It applies to the Website, our emails and our customer support channels.

It does not cover other websites that we link to. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.

3. Information we collect

We collect only the information we need to run the store and look after you. Depending on how you use the Website this may include:

  1. contact details such as your name, email address, delivery address and phone number;
  2. order details such as the products you bought, the date of purchase and delivery instructions;
  3. account details such as your password (stored in hashed form) and saved preferences;
  4. technical information such as your browser type, device type and the pages you visited, collected through essential cookies;
  5. messages you send to our support team.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to process and deliver your orders, to answer your questions, to keep the Website secure, to prevent fraud and to meet our legal obligations such as keeping tax records.

If you choose to join our newsletter we will send you occasional emails about new products. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing does not affect your account or your orders.

5. Who we work with

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We share information only with the service providers who help us run the store, such as our payment processor, our hosting provider and the delivery companies that bring your parcel to your door, and only to the extent they need it to do that job.

These providers are bound by contracts that require them to keep your information confidential and secure and to use it only on our instructions. We may also disclose information where the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order.

6. Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies to keep you signed in, remember what is in your basket and protect the Website against abuse. We use privacy-friendly analytics that does not track you across other websites. You can block cookies in your browser settings, although some parts of the Website may then stop working.

7. How long we keep information

We keep order records for seven years because tax law requires it. We keep account information for as long as your account is open and delete it within 90 days of you closing your account. Support messages are deleted after two years.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to certain uses and to ask us to transfer it to another organisation. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@fairbox.example. We will not charge you for this and we will answer within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you also have the right to complain to your local data protection regulator.

9. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, access controls and regular security reviews. Card numbers are handled by our payment processor and never touch our servers.

10. International transfers

Our servers are located in the United States. If you visit the Website from another country, your information will be processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country. Where we transfer information internationally we use safeguards recognised by law, such as standard contractual clauses.

11. No advertising tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not build advertising profiles about you. We measure how the Website is used with an analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not record your IP address.

12. Changes to this policy

We review this policy at least once a year. If we make a significant change to how we use your information we will tell you by email or with a notice on the Website before the change takes effect, and the date at the top of this page will always show when it was last updated.

13. Children

The Website is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

14. Contact us

If you have any questions about this document or anything else, you can email us at hello@fairbox.example. We aim to reply within two business days. You can also write to us at PrivacyOnly Home Goods Ltd., 100 Example Street, Portland, Oregon.
