This Returns Policy explains how to send back a product you bought from Gadgetly. It forms part of our Terms of Sale.

1. About us

Gadgetly is a trading name of Gadgetly Retail LLC, a company registered in Austin, Texas. When these documents refer to "Gadgetly", "we", "us" or "our", they mean Gadgetly Retail LLC and, where the context requires, its employees and authorised agents.

We have been operating our online store since 2019 and we are proud of the community of customers who have trusted us along the way. Our team is small, and the people who answer your emails are the same people who choose what we stock and write these pages.

2. Definitions

In this document the following words have the meanings given below. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. Words in the singular include the plural and the other way round.

  1. "Website" means the Gadgetly website and any pages, tools and content made available on it.
  2. "Customer", "you" and "your" mean the person using the Website or placing an order with us.
  3. "Order" means a request by you to buy one or more products or services from us through the Website.
  4. "Business day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the state where we are registered.
  5. "Content" means text, images, video, software, logos and other material published on the Website.

3. Return window

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a refund to your original payment method. Items must be returned in their original packaging with all accessories, manuals and cables, and must not show signs of use beyond what is needed to inspect them.

Some items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons once their seal is broken, such as in-ear tips and personal grooming devices. These are clearly marked on the product page.

4. Restocking fee

A restocking fee of 25% of the purchase price will be deducted from the refund for every returned item.

The restocking fee covers inspecting, cleaning, repackaging and testing returned products before they can be sold again. It is shown on your return confirmation email.

5. Return shipping

You are responsible for the cost of return shipping on all returns, including items you no longer want.

We recommend that you use a tracked service, because we cannot issue a refund for parcels that do not reach our returns centre. Original delivery charges are not refunded.

6. How to start a return

To start a return:

  1. sign in to your Gadgetly account and open Order history;
  2. choose the item you would like to return and select a reason;
  3. print the return form and place it inside the parcel;
  4. send the parcel to the address shown on the return form.

7. Refund timing

Once your return arrives we inspect it within 5 business days. If it passes inspection, we issue the refund, less any restocking fee, to your original payment method. Your bank may take a further 3 to 10 business days to show the money in your account.

8. Faulty and damaged items

If an item arrives damaged or develops a fault within the manufacturer's warranty period, please contact us before returning it. We will arrange a repair or replacement under the manufacturer's warranty. Your legal rights in relation to faulty goods are not affected by this policy.

9. Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. If you would like a different colour or model, please return the original item and place a new order.

10. Product information

We try hard to describe and photograph our products accurately. Colours can look slightly different on different screens, and handmade or natural materials vary a little from piece to piece. Measurements are given as a guide and may vary by a small amount.

If we discover an obvious error in a product description after you have ordered, we will contact you to explain and ask how you would like to proceed before we ship anything.
