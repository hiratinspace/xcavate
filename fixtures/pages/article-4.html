Streaming prices keep climbing

Another streaming service raised its prices this week. The basic tier now costs $9.99 a month, up from $7.99, and the ad-free tier rises to $17.99/month for new customers from next month.

Analysts say the increases reflect higher licensing costs. Several services have also ended their free trials, and one now charges $4.99 per month for an extra member outside the household.

Consumer groups advise checking renewal dates and cancellation terms before a trial converts. Most services let customers cancel online, although some still require a phone call.
